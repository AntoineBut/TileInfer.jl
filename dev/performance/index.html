Performance

What each feature of the engine gains, measured on the RTX 4080 SUPER (736 GB/s), in BFloat16 unless noted, on Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3-4B. How the features work is described in Architecture, KV cache, Attention kernels and Speculative decoding; the order they were built in is in History and roadmap. Variants that were measured and not kept are listed under each feature, with the measurement, so they are not retried blindly. What is left to gain is collected at the end.

How it is measured

  • bench/run.jl (see CLAUDE.md): minimum time over samples, --seconds 2; expect ±3–5 % between runs at the model level (the GPU also drives the desktop). Model benchmarks:
    • decode_ctx1024 / decode_graph_ctx1024: one decode step after 1,023 cached positions, eager or replayed from a CUDA graph, synchronized;
    • generate64[_unpipelined|_sampled]_ctx1024: 64 tokens after 1,023 positions, per token; pipelined greedy, greedy step by step, and the model's recommended sampling (Qwen3: temperature 0.6, top-k 20, top-p 0.95), pipelined;
    • prefill64, prefill512: a prompt in one step.
    The % of roofline counts every weight array once, the embedding table included.
  • bench/breakdown.jl: GPU time per kernel inside real steps (decode replayed from graphs), by role (see Profiling).
  • bench/speculative.jl: 256-token chat replies to fixed prompts; decode tok/s from the first new token to the last; best of 3.
  • The vLLM race (bench/ref/, see its README): fresh processes alternating the two engines, medians of every trial after a 1 s warm-up. Without the warm-up, the GPU runs the first ~0.2 s at 900–1,600 MHz instead of 2,775 MHz, and a 10 ms request takes 16–40 ms.
  • Decode roofline at 1,024 positions: Qwen3-0.6B reads 1.19 GB of weights + 117 MB of KV cache per token, 1.78 ms (~560 tok/s); Qwen3-4B 8.04 GB + 151 MB, 11.1 ms (~90 tok/s).

Prefill GEMMs

Per-shape tiles (gemm_config): the tile minimizing waves × (TM TN)^0.7 among 6 shapes. Fitted to a sweep of 30 tile shapes × 8 layers (both models) × n = 2…1,024: within 2 % of the best tile on average (worst 1.18×), 1.22× the previous rule (64 or 128 rows × the next power of two ≥ n columns). On 0.6B at n = 64: o 1.77×, down 1.98×, prefill64 3.62 → 3.00 ms.

Two blocks per SM (gemm_occupancy): occupancy = 2 on grids of ≥ 160 blocks. Applied everywhere, it slowed the small grids down:

INT8 weights

Weight-only INT8 with one scale per output channel (quantize = :int8, --int8), the embedding in BF16. Accuracy, mean KL divergence to the BF16 model over the fixture prompts and top-1 agreement: Qwen3-0.6B 0.032 (46/50), Qwen3-4B 0.009 (49/50). Apertus 1.5 8B in INT8 against transformers' Float32 run of the unquantized checkpoint: the argmax differs at 2 of 103 positions, both near-ties (margins 0.2 and 0.02), and 24-token greedy continuations are identical on all three prompts.

Apertus 8B in INT8 holds 8.6 GB of weights (linear 7.0 GB, lm_head 0.54 GB, BF16 embedding 1.07 GB) and loads in 12 s. Its decode step (12.4 ms from a graph) reads ~7.5 GB of weights plus the KV cache, ~82 % of the roofline.

GEMV tiles. TK × TM ≈ 4,096 Int8 elements (TK = 1,024, TM = 4), half BF16's width, was best on every Apertus 8B layer; BF16's TM = 8 was 7–13 % slower, TK ≥ 2,048 with TM ≥ 4 much slower. The INT8 GEMVs run at 85–93 % of the roofline (Apertus: qkv 91 %, o 87 %, up + xIELU 91 %, down 85 %, lm_head 93 %).

GEMM tiles. With BF16's tiles, INT8 prefill was 8–11 % slower than BF16. Under ncu (Qwen3-4B down): at 512 tokens 255 registers and 1.6× BF16's instructions (byte shuffles and conversions of the weight tile); at 64 tokens 29 % of DRAM peak against BF16's 90 %, with 37 % of the stall samples on the conversion waiting for its global load, because the Int8 tile is not staged through shared memory like the BF16 operands. A sweep of 15 tiles × occupancy 1, 2 on the layers of Qwen3-0.6B, Qwen3-4B and Apertus 8B at n = 8, 64, 512 found that INT8 blocks always want two per SM, narrower tiles than BF16 at 512 tokens (128 × 64 × 64), and deeper K steps at ≤ 64 tokens (32 × 32 × 256, or 64 × 64 × 128 on large grids). gemm_int8_config is within 1.5 % of the best tile summed over the sweep (worst 1.11×).
